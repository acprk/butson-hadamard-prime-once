\section*{Use of AI tools}
Parts of this work were carried out with the assistance of AI systems,
which were used to explore arguments, to draft and revise parts of the
text, to write computer programs for the computations, and to write
parts of the Lean formalization. All mathematical statements and proofs
in this paper have been checked by the authors. The statements listed in
the first part of Table~\ref{tab:lean} have been verified in Lean~4,
with the scope indicated there, and the numerical
results have been reproduced by independent programs. The authors take
full responsibility for the content of the paper.
