We prove that if a prime $p$ divides the order of a finite abelian group
$G$ exactly once and is unramified in $\Q(\zeta_h)$, then there is no
$G$-invariant Butson Hadamard matrix $\BH(G,h)$; equivalently, no
generalized bent function $G\to\Z_h$ and, for cyclic $G$, no perfect
sequence of length $|G|$ over $\mu_h$. The proof uses the local ring
$\F[T]/(T-1)^p$ and Parseval's identity on a single coset of the Sylow
$p$-subgroup. Consequences: the conjectures of Mow and of Do Duc hold
for square-free lengths; the case $m=1$ of the Kumar--Scholtz--Welch
problem on generalized bent functions $\Z_q^m\to\Z_q$, $q\equiv2\pmod4$,
is settled; the forbidden subgroup of a semi-regular abelian relative
difference set is a $p$-group whenever $p$ divides its index exactly
once; and the eleven orders below $1000$ left open in Arasu's survey of
circulant complex Hadamard matrices are excluded. For the family of
perfect $p$-ary sequences of length $pq^e$, to which the theorem does not
apply when $p$ is odd and $e\ge2$, we prove nonexistence for all $e$,
using a comparison lemma at the ramified prime $p$; for $34$ of the
$334$ such lengths up to $2000$ no earlier criterion we know of applies.
The main theorem, the comparison lemma (for integer $n$) and the nonexistence of perfect
$p$-ary sequences of length $pq^e$ are formalized in Lean~4.
